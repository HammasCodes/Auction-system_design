01 / The big picture

Four moving parts

The mobile app is what people see. Everything else sits behind it. The backend is the brain: it holds the rules, the products, the bids and the money logic.

A

The app

One React Native app for buyers and sellers. It asks the backend for everything it shows.

B

The backend

The rules live here. Who can sell, what a bid is worth, who won, who gets paid.

C

The record book

A permanent, ordered record. Nothing about money is ever guessed or estimated.

D

The partners

Stripe handles money and ID checks. We do not store card numbers or ID documents.

02 / Getting sellers on

How a seller becomes verified

Two gates. Stripe checks that the person or business is real. Then your team decides if they belong on the platform.

GATE 1

Stripe verifies

Legal name, business details, bank account. Stripe is the same service used by thousands of marketplaces, so this part is proven and we do not hold sensitive documents.

GATE 2

You approve

Your team sees a queue of pending sellers in an admin panel and approves or rejects each one. This keeps the platform curated, which was the goal.

ONGOING

You can pause anyone

A seller can be suspended at any time. Their live auctions stop, and pending payouts are held until the issue is resolved.

03 / One auction, start to finish

What actually happens

Follow a single item from the moment a seller photographs it to the moment the money lands in their bank.

01

Seller lists the item

Photos, title, starting price, and how long the auction runs. Photos go straight to fast storage so the app loads quickly anywhere in the world.

02

The auction goes live

A countdown starts. Everyone watching sees the same clock and the same current price, updated the instant anything changes.

03

Buyers bid

Each bid is checked before it counts: is the auction still open, is the amount high enough, is the buyer allowed to bid. Only then does the price move.

04

Last second bids extend the clock

If someone bids in the final seconds, the timer adds a little more time. This stops sniping and keeps auctions fair, which keeps sellers happy.

05

The auction closes and the winner pays

The system picks the winning bid, creates the order, and charges the winner. If the payment fails, the order is cancelled and the item can be relisted.

06

The money is held, not sent

The payment sits safely with Stripe. The seller can see it is coming, but cannot spend it yet. This is what protects buyers.

07

Seller ships and adds tracking

The buyer gets a notification with the tracking number and can follow the parcel from inside the app.

08

Delivery confirmed, payout released

Once delivery is confirmed, the hold is lifted. Your commission is taken automatically and the rest goes to the seller on the payout schedule.

REFUNDS

If something goes wrong

While money is held, a refund is simple and instant. This is exactly why funds are not released the moment a buyer pays.

PAYOUTS

On a schedule

Sellers are paid on a fixed cycle rather than per sale. Fewer transfers means lower fees and a clear statement for each seller.

RECORDS

Every cent is traceable

Each order stores what the buyer paid, what you kept, what the seller earned, and when it moved. Nothing is calculated after the fact.

05 / The thing you asked about

Two people bid at the same second

You asked what a race condition is. This is it, and this is how we stop it from ever costing you money.

Without this

Both bids could be accepted. Two people think they won the same item. One gets charged for nothing, and you deal with the complaint.

With this

Bids form a single line, like a queue at a counter. One is served at a time, so the result is always clean and always explainable.

Same idea for payments

A payment confirmation is only ever applied once, even if the message arrives twice. No accidental double charges or double payouts.

HONEST NOTE

About the one to two week target

The core auction flow is reachable in that window. Payments and payouts depend on Stripe account setup and identity approval, which is partly out of our hands. The realistic plan is a working, testable product fast, with the money side finished as soon as accounts are live.

HOW WE AVOID SURPRISES

You see it as it is built

Each stage is testable before the mobile app is finished, so nothing waits on anything else. If a decision changes mid build, we adjust at the next stage instead of rewriting.

09 / Needed from you

Three decisions before we start

Everything else is settled. These three shape the money side and cannot be guessed.

DECISION 01

Which countries at launch

This decides which sellers can be paid and which currencies buyers see. Starting with fewer countries means shipping sooner. We can add more later.

DECISION 02

The commission rate

A single flat percentage on each sale is simplest and easiest to explain to sellers. Tiered rates can come later once there is volume to reward.

DECISION 03

Accounts in your name

Stripe, hosting and the domain should be created under the business. It keeps ownership clean from day one and avoids a painful handover later.

ALSO WORTH KNOWING

Your team needs a small web panel

Approving sellers and handling disputes does not fit inside a phone app. A simple internal web page covers it, and it is included in the plan.

RUNNING COST

Modest at launch

At early volumes the monthly cost sits in the low hundreds. It grows with usage rather than being paid for up front, so nothing is spent ahead of demand.
